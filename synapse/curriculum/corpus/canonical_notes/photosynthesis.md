# Photosynthesis: Light and Dark Reactions

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy in plants and algae.

## Light-Dependent Reactions
Light-dependent reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes of chloroplasts. Chlorophyll absorbs photons, exciting electrons that travel through an electron transport chain. This process photolyzes water molecules, producing oxygen gas, ATP, and NADPH.

## Calvin Cycle (Light-Independent Reactions)
The Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma of chloroplasts. The enzyme RuBisCO catalyzes carbon fixation by combining carbon dioxide with ribulose 1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP). Using ATP and NADPH from the light reactions, 3-phosphoglycerate is reduced to glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate (G3P), which is used to synthesize glucose.
