# Taste Skill — Aesthetic Refinement & Anti-Slop Visual Direction

> Purpose: Visual taste, style direction, aesthetic refinement, composition, and visual balance.
> Enforces human-designed, believable, polished, and purposeful aesthetics while strictly prohibiting generic AI-generated templates.

## 0. BRIEF & VIBE INFERENCE
Before modifying or styling UI components:
1. **Infer Purpose & Audience**: Educational / Classroom diagnostic learning architecture for teachers and students.
2. **Desired Vibe**:
   - Clean, modern, elegant, human-designed, educational, professional, restrained, believable, polished.
   - Quiet, confident visual balance.
3. **Anti-Default Prohibitions (STRICTLY PROHIBITED)**:
   - NO AI-purple gradients across hero containers.
   - NO excessive neon glows, cybernetic borders, or particle networks.
   - NO futuristic HUD / sci-fi dashboard motifs.
   - NO overused glassmorphism on every card with blinding borders.
   - NO infinite-loop distracting micro-animations or light sweeps.
   - NO generic 3-card marketing template layouts where not applicable.

## 1. THE THREE DIALS
- **`DESIGN_VARIANCE: 4`** (Balanced, structured, professional, clean layout)
- **`MOTION_INTENSITY: 2`** (Restrained, subtle transitions: 150-200ms ease-out, zero bouncy theatrics)
- **`VISUAL_DENSITY: 5`** (Balanced information hierarchy: readable typography, clear data tables/cards)

## 2. COLOR DIRECTION
- **Surface**: Pure, deep dark or crisp slate surfaces with subtle natural tone separation rather than neon glows.
- **Accents**: Intentional, functional accents (e.g. focused educational slate/indigo, clear emerald for mastery, amber for review, rose for gaps).
- **Contrast**: Clear text hierarchy (high-contrast primary headings, readable body text, muted metadata with minimum 4.5:1 ratio).

## 3. CARD & COMPONENT COMPOSITION
- Flat or subtle 1px hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or refined neutral).
- Clean, tactile elevation on hover (`translateY(-2px)` max, gentle shadow).
- Clear typography scale with generous line-height (1.5 for body, 1.2 for headings).
